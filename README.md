# 🚀 Overview

AnimaSys ERP is a modern enterprise management platform designed for pet stores, veterinary clinics, and pet service businesses.

The platform centralizes inventory, sales, employee management, customer records, appointments, AI insights, and business analytics into one intelligent dashboard.
